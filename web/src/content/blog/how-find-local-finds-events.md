If you've ever searched "things to do tonight" and gotten the same arena tour and stadium show on every site, you've seen the gap Find Local was built for. Most event platforms list what ticketing companies sell. But the majority of what actually happens in a city — the jazz trio at a neighborhood bar, the Tuesday open mic, the drag brunch, the community theater run — never touches a big ticketing pipeline.

## Straight from the source

Find Local works differently: we go to the venues themselves. Our pipeline reads the calendars of thousands of local venues — music halls, comedy clubs, theaters, bars, community spaces — across 47 US cities, and turns them into one structured, searchable feed that updates daily.

That means the listings include the shows that only ever existed as a line on a venue's own website. No account required, no markup, no "fees revealed at checkout" — tickets link straight to the venue or its own ticketing page.

## Browse it your way

- **[The event feed](/)** shows everything upcoming in your city, filterable by date, category, price, and time of day.
- **The map view** puts every venue with upcoming events on a map, so you can see what's walkable tonight.
- **[Venue pages](/venues)** collect each spot's upcoming calendar in one place.
- **City pages** like [Boston](/city/boston), [New York](/city/new-york), and [Chicago](/city/chicago) give you a quick date-grouped rundown of what's coming up.

## For builders

The same structured event data behind the site is available to developers through the [FindLocal platform](/platform): a free [JSON API](/blog/findlocal-events-api) and an [MCP server](/blog/findlocal-mcp-server) you can plug into Claude, Cursor, and other AI assistants.

Find Local is free to use — pick your city and see what's on tonight at [findlocal.community](/).
